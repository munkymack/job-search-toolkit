# Portfolio context: template

Optional. For any field where your work lives somewhere a reader can click: a portfolio site, GitHub, case studies, a writing archive, a deck library.

A link alone isn't enough, for the AI or for a hiring manager. The AI can't reliably browse your site, and even when it can, it doesn't know what you're proud of, what you'd cut, or what each piece was for. This file tells it.

Aim for 6 to 10 entries, in the order they appear on your site.

```markdown
# Portfolio context: [Your name]

Portfolio URL: [link]

## Featured work (in the order shown)

### [Project name]
- Client or employer:
- Year:
- My role: (lead, contributor, sole owner, etc.)
- Format: (campaign, product, report, analysis, talk, code, etc.)
- The brief: 1 sentence. What they asked for.
- The insight: 1 sentence. What made it work.
- The result: 1 sentence. A measurable outcome if you have one. If not, say "no data."
- What I'm proud of: 1 sentence. For your own use.

### [Next project]
[same structure]
```

The "what I'm proud of" line is for the AI's benefit, not the site's. It teaches the AI what you value in your work, which is what makes outreach and letters sound genuine. Be honest. If the answer is "the constraint we solved," say that.

Every entry should also exist as a story block if you plan to make claims from it. The portfolio context is the map. The blocks are the checked claims.
