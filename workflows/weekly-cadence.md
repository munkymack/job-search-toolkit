# Workflow: the weekly cadence

A structured week: two hours a day, five days, each day with one job. It exists to rebalance effort away from cold board applications and toward relationships and visibility, where senior roles tend to move.

Adjust the hours to your life. Keep the rhythm: one kind of work per day, and a hard stop on Friday.

## The diagnosis it answers

| Pain point | Root cause | Antidote |
|---|---|---|
| Ghosting, no responses | Over-reliance on cold board applications | Shift most of your effort to network and warm outreach |
| Repetitive tailoring | No modular content | Build the story block library once |
| Hard to find good roles | Pipeline depends on what's publicly listed | Build a target-company list. Many senior roles fill before posting. |

## At a glance

| Day | Focus | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Pipeline and targeting | 8 to 15 roles collected, target list refreshed |
| Tuesday | Deep application day | 4 or 5 tailored applications |
| Wednesday | Network and warm outreach | 5 messages plus LinkedIn engagement |
| Thursday | Craft and portfolio | One portfolio improvement or interview prep |
| Friday | Review, follow-up, reset | Follow-ups sent, week reviewed, weekend off |

## Monday: pipeline and targeting

- **Scan boards and collect roles. 75 minutes. Don't apply yet.** Use the ATS search console (`tools/ats-search-console/`), LinkedIn Jobs, and the boards specific to your field. Every role worth another look goes to capture-jd immediately.
- **Build or refresh your target-company list. 45 minutes.** Twenty to thirty places you'd want to work, hiring or not. See `templates/target-companies-template.md`.
- Tracker columns to fill for each role you note: company, role, link, deadline, fit (1 to 5), who you'd want to talk to there.

## Tuesday: deep application day

Four or five applications, about 25 minutes each. Tailored only. Skip anything not worth 25 minutes.

Per application: ten minutes on the cover letter (from story blocks), ten on resume tailoring and portfolio order, five finding a human at the company to message on Wednesday. Use the application loop (`workflows/tailor-resume.md`), with recruiter-screen up front.

**Rule:** if a role doesn't earn 25 minutes of effort, don't apply. Four thoughtful applications beat twenty generic ones.

## Wednesday: network and warm outreach

Highest return, most skipped. If you only have 90 minutes one week, spend them here.

- **Five messages, 60 minutes.** Rotate the mix: a former colleague, a senior person you admire, a specialized recruiter, a weak-tie reconnect, and someone at a target company. See `workflows/linkedin-outreach.md` and `workflows/warm-network-mining.md`.
- **LinkedIn engagement, 60 minutes.** Five to ten substantive comments and one post of your own. "Great post!" doesn't count. Comment on people at your target companies. Your own post: a teardown, a hot take, or a craft observation. **Not an "open to work" post,** which can cap how senior people perceive you.

## Thursday: craft and portfolio

Rotate through four weeks:

| Week | Mode | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Refresh an existing piece | Sharper framing or results |
| 2 | Create a new spec piece | A fictional brief, teardown, or rewrite |
| 3 | Interview prep | Five STAR stories, your philosophy of the work in 90 seconds, your point of view on AI, how you give feedback, how you partner with adjacent functions, three questions that prove you've researched the company |
| 4 | Repeat week 1 | The cycle continues |

If your field doesn't have a portfolio, use this day for the equivalent: a case study, a teardown of a product you'd work on, a written analysis. Interview prep is the same for everyone.

## Friday: review, follow up, reset

- **Follow-ups, 45 minutes.** Run pipeline-sweep, then draft-followup for the ones worth it. Applications seven or more days old with no response, outreach seven or more days old.
- **Week review, 45 minutes.** Use the weekly retro prompt in `prompts-library.md`.
- **Hard log-off, 30 minutes.** Buffer to actually disconnect.

**Follow-up rules:** portal silence after seven days means message a person at the company directly. Outreach silence after seven days gets one gentle bump, then move on. Two bumps without a reply, drop it.

**Weekly review questions** (answer them in your tracker):
- Shots on goal this week: applications, outreach, posts and comments
- What got responses?
- Which channel is underperforming and should I drop it?
- One thing to do differently next week

## Breaking through the malaise

| Trigger | Reframe | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Ghosting | Mostly company dysfunction (frozen reqs, internal candidates), not you | Track shots on goal, not goals. Quality actions equal a successful day. |
| Repetitive tailoring | You're rewriting when you should be assembling | Build the story block library |
| Thin pipeline | You're fishing where the senior roles aren't | Target-company list plus warm outreach |

Built-in wins, because the search won't give you any: one coffee chat a week with someone in your field, a Friday "what I made this week" ritual, one public piece shipped per month.

## Research topics worth an hour each

Your field's market state (consolidation, layoffs, AI restructuring), how AI is changing your role (interviewers will ask, and you need a real point of view), alternative paths into your field, compensation benchmarks for negotiation, and cultural literacy in your field (what the people interviewing you read).

## How the system was first run

The system's first run designed the schedule in week one and built the infrastructure (story blocks, target list, tracker, tools) before running it, then switched the cadence on. If you can't build first, do Monday and Wednesday. They carry the most weight.
