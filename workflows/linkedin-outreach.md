# Workflow: LinkedIn outreach

A short message to one real person. Research first, draft second.

## The rules

- **75 to 150 words.**
- **Open specifically only if there's something real** (a post, a project, a shared employer). Otherwise be direct. A forced "I saw your post" is worse than none.
- **No call to action by default.** Open the door. Let them decide.
- **Research before writing.** Share what you found, then draft. Light touch: two or three things, not a dossier. Go deeper only if it's a person who matters a lot.
- Never mention being between jobs.
- Only claims that exist in your story blocks.
- Run the cleanup pass.

## Who to write to (a weekly mix)

From the weekly cadence: one former colleague, one senior person you admire (cold, but warm-toned), one recruiter who specializes in your field, one weak-tie reconnect you haven't spoken to in a year or two, and one person at a company from your target list. Rotate. Five messages a week is a useful default.

## The sequence

1. Pick the person (from your warm list, your target list, or a role you're applying to).
2. Run the research prompt below.
3. Read the findings. Correct anything wrong.
4. Draft.
5. Cleanup pass.
6. Send it yourself.
7. Log it in an outreach tracker (see the bottom of this file).

## Paste-ready: research

```
I'm going to message [NAME], [TITLE] at [COMPANY]. Before drafting, research them lightly.

Give me, in under 150 words:
- What they own or work on, based on their public profile and any recent post, talk, or article.
- One real, specific thing I could reference. If there isn't one, say "nothing specific," and I'll write a direct message.
- Anything we share (employer, school, mutual connection) that's visible from what I gave you.
- What I still don't know.

Use web search if available. Date any recent item. Do not invent shared history. If you can't verify something, say so.

What I already know about them: [paste anything]
```

## Paste-ready: draft

```
Draft a LinkedIn message to [NAME]. Reason for writing: [applying to X / want to learn about the team / reconnecting].

Rules:
- 75 to 150 words.
- Open with the specific thing only if it's real and from the research. Otherwise open directly.
- No call to action. Open the door and stop.
- Use only claims from my story blocks. One sentence about me, maximum two.
- Not allowed: "pick your brain," "circling back," "hope this finds you well," "I'd love to connect."
- Sign as [first name].

Give me the message in a fenced code block. Then one line on why you opened the way you did.

Research findings: [paste]
My relevant background: [paste, or "use my story blocks"]
```

## Outreach tracker (minimal)

Keep a second tab or table with:

| Name | Title / Company | Relationship (colleague / cold / recruiter / weak tie) | Channel (LinkedIn / email) | Date sent | Replied? | Notes | Next action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Follow-up rules: silence after seven days gets one gentle bump. Two bumps without a reply means you drop it, no hard feelings.
